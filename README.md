# Happy 19th Birthday Website for Dilsha ♡

A romantic, 8-page mobile-first birthday website handcrafted with love for Dilsha.

## ✨ Features Included:
1. **Page 1 — The Surprise Question**: Cute pastel-pink scrapbook camera frame with Dilsha's portrait, stickers, and interactive YES/NO buttons (with playful modal if NO is tapped).
2. **Page 2 — Unlock My Heart**: Burgundy-to-pink velvet gradient, glowing heart lock, floating rose petals, and realistic numeric keypad (PIN: **1207**).

4. **Page 4 — My Love Letter**: Vintage cream parchment letter with burgundy wax seal, satin ribbon, and handwritten calligraphy.
5. **Page 5 — Our Beautiful Memories**: Interactive Polaroid-style slideshow with all 6 of your real couple photos, captions, and titles.
6. **Page 6 — Why You Are Special**: 5 glowing cards detailing the heartfelt reasons why she is irreplaceable.
7. **Page 7 — Happy 19th Birthday**: Elegant birthday cake with candles you can tap to blow out, wishing overlay, and celebratory golden confetti.
8. **Page 8 — The Final Surprise**: Grand finale revealing an exquisite sparkling diamond ring inside a luxury burgundy velvet box, with your forever vow.
- **Romantic Music Soundtrack**: Plays your custom soundtrack (`music main.mp3`) by default on load with looping, auto-play gesture support, and interactive toggle.
- **Floating Rose Petals**: Smooth physics-based 2.5D falling rose petals on canvas.
